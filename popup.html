A separate model reads 61 signals from the same data: accuracy, results, move timing, activity and how games end. Rating itself is left out. The overlay names the signals that raised the score.

Record only: 35% win rate, 30% recent win rate, 35% accuracy, times an age factor (×5 under 7 days, down to ×1 after a year).

Disclaimer

A risk indicator, not proof of cheating. A high score means unusual patterns worth a look. Give players the benefit of the doubt, and report suspected cheaters through official Chess.com or Lichess channels.

Privacy

No data collection and no tracking. All analysis runs locally on public Chess.com and Lichess data. Signed in to Lichess, requests carry your login because Lichess answers signed-in users faster. The My stats page asks Chess.com and Lichess for public data only when you press a button on that page, and keeps the results in this browser. Skills analyses your games on this computer with the Stockfish chess engine (GPL-3.0, see vendor/stockfish); your games are not sent anywhere. Report does nothing until you press it, then Lichess asks you to confirm. Open source.
